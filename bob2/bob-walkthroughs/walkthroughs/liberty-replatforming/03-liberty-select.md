# 2. Select "Liberty Replatforming"

![Image](03-img.png)

Before beginning the replatforming process, Bob analyzes your workspace and tailors the experience to your specific needs. Select the options that best match your requirements.

Choose "Liberty Replatforming" to initiate the transformation process and provide your [Application Modernization Accelerator](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/ama?topic=accelerator-application-modernization) deployment plan ZIP file. Bob will then begin the comprehensive migration of your application to the Liberty platform.
